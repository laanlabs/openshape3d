# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help.

**Issues are welcome.** Bug reports and feature requests are the best way to
contribute. For a bug, say what you did, what happened and what you expected,
and attach a design (.os3d) or the steps that reproduce it. For a feature,
describe how it should behave from the user's side. Measurements and
screenshots help. One issue per bug or feature, please.

**Pull requests are not merged.** OpenShape 3D ships in the App Store, and
everything in that build is written and reviewed inside the project. Please
do not open pull requests; describe the change in an issue instead. This is
a rule about what ships, not a judgement of anyone's code.

**Describe the behaviour rather than link code.** We re-implement from the
report and prefer not to work from an outside implementation, so an issue
that explains what a feature does is more useful to us than a link to a
branch or fork that does it.

The source is open under the licence in this repository, and forks are
welcome.
